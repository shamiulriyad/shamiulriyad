Unresolved questions

  1. Novelty. Do published or patented methods already combine per-die reliability, weighted fusion and explicit abstention? Industrial multi-die median and computed references exist [15–17].
  2. Real data. Can a lab or industry partner provide 4–5-die same-design SEM groups with verified clean or design-rendered references, under what licence or NDA?
  3. Registration on real SEM. Is sub-pixel accuracy reachable under charging, drift and scan distortion? How often do periodic layouts cause one-pitch errors?
  4. Process variation. Will normal die-to-die variation be flagged as defects, cutting support and coverage?
  5. Detector transfer (H5). How far do U-Net, PatchCore and PaDiM trained on Carinthia-S, MVTec or synthetic data transfer to the target layer?
  6. Repeated defects. Without design data, how can defects shared by all dies be detected at all?
  7. Thresholds. Best s_min, τ_r, κ and leakage tolerance τ_ℓ; to be set on validation designs.
  8. Synthetic realism. Which defect and imaging models make synthetic rankings predictive of real ones?
